Prompt Dungeon

You build the dungeon. Other players die in it.

Type one sentence. The game reads your words, builds a dungeon floor out of them — theme, monsters, hazards and rules — then gives you 60 seconds to fight through it.

NEW⚡ DASH🔥 COMBO x7❤️❤️❤️❤️ 4 HEARTS💚 HEALING LAVA✦ BIGGER ENEMIES

Or tap an example:

Move: WASD or arrow keys  ·  Shoot: mouse click / hold, or Space  ·  Dash: Shift (briefly invulnerable, 2s cooldown)
On touch screens: left stick to move, big red FIRE button to shoot (it aims for you), big teal DASH button next to it to dash — watch its ring refill. Double-tap the arena also dashes.
Win: clear every enemy, or open the loot chest and survive the full 60 seconds. You have 4 hearts. Chain kills within 3 seconds to build a combo multiplier, and grab the hearts enemies sometimes drop.
Keywords matter: try boss, dark, slow, fog, robots, pirates, ice, storm… the dungeon obeys the words it recognises.
